FAQS

Everything you need to know.

Clear answers about pricing, domain mapping, payments, and store ownership.

Frequently Asked Questions 8 Answers
Is it really ₹99 per month?

Yes. The Base plan is ₹99 per month and includes everything you need to sell online — 15 themes, unlimited products, orders, categories, discount codes, and analytics. No hidden fees or sales commissions.

Do I need coding skills?

Not at all. You can set up your store in minutes by selecting a theme, typing your store details, and uploading your products. Everything is managed from an intuitive, clean visual dashboard.

Can I sell products other than clothing?

Yes! MetoHub is designed for any type of product: fashion, groceries, gadgets, jewelry, watches, perfume, artisanal crafts, cosmetics, home decor, and more. Our themes adapt seamlessly to your niche.

Can I personalize the theme and store content?

Absolutely. You can choose from 15 themes, upload your custom logo, customize brand colors, add homepage banners, highlight featured collections, and configure promotional banners anytime.

How do customers pay for orders?

Cash on delivery (COD) and manual payments are built right in, perfect for direct seller-to-buyer sales with zero gateway fees. For your MetoHub subscription itself, payments are securely processed via Razorpay (UPI, Cards, Net Banking).

Can I use my own domain?

Yes! On the Pro (₹249/mo) and Enterprise (₹749/mo) plans, you can connect your own custom domain (e.g. yourstore.com or yourbrand.in) with automatic free SSL security.

What happens after the free trial?

You enjoy a 7-day free trial on all plans. If you do not subscribe immediately after the trial, your storefront remains visible and active for an additional 30 days so your customers never experience downtime while you decide.

What do I need to create a store?

Just your name, email, phone number, business name, and password. No credit card is required to begin your 7-day free trial.
